Party Spy · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026 · Made by Sumix Developers

Party Spy is a party game. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

Player names, missions you write or edit, scores, badges and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data) deletes them.

Private rooms

If you choose to play in a private room (everyone on their own phone), the nickname you type and the current game (missions, votes, scores) are sent to the other phones in that room through Supabase Realtime, a hosting service we use for this. Rooms are not stored: the data is passed between phones while the game is on and is gone when everyone leaves. The connection is encrypted. Pass-the-phone play sends nothing at all.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for private rooms and sharing.
  • Vibrate: for haptic feedback during the game.
  • Keep awake: so the screen stays on while the round timer runs.
  • Audio settings: so sound effects play correctly.

The app does not use your microphone, camera, photos, location, contacts or files.

Sharing

When you share results, the app hands the text or picture to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). What happens next is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Children

Party Spy is meant for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
